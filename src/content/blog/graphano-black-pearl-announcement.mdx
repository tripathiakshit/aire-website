AI Resource Exploration (AIRE) is pleased to announce its engagement by **Graphano Energy Ltd.** (TSXV: GEL | FSE: 97G0 | OTC Pink: GELEF) to support the advancement of the company's Black Pearl graphite project located in Québec, Canada. AIRE's mandate is to apply its proprietary **CAGE-IN** technology to integrate geophysical, geological, and historical exploration datasets, with the objective of delineating and ranking the most prospective zones ahead of ground follow-up and first-phase drilling.

Graphano's recent airborne geophysical surveys — including magnetic and time-domain electromagnetic (TDEM) data — have identified multiple discrete conductive zones along a strike length exceeding six kilometres. These conductors represent priority targets for graphite mineralization, and the complexity and scale of the dataset make it an ideal candidate for CAGE-IN's multi-layer integration framework.

> "The identification of multiple conductors over a significant strike length is highly encouraging. By incorporating advanced data-driven analysis, we aim to efficiently prioritize the most prospective targets and deploy our field programs with greater precision."
>
> **Dr. Luisa Moreno, Chief Executive Officer, Graphano Energy Ltd.**

## Why CAGE-IN for Black Pearl

The Black Pearl project presents a multi-conductor geophysical environment across a laterally extensive trend — precisely the setting where conventional processing falls short. Standard geophysical gridding does not account for geological domain boundaries, which can distort anomaly geometry and produce misleading target geometries. CAGE-IN resolves this by imposing geological constraints directly onto the geophysical data, ensuring that processing occurs strictly within appropriate geological domains.

For graphite — a conductive mineral whose signature can be masked or blended by surrounding lithologies — this geological constraint is critical to distinguishing true mineralization from geophysical artefacts. CAGE-IN's ability to enhance subtle anomaly contrast, combined with its multi-dataset relational scanning, allows AIRE to define high-confidence targets that a conventional workflow would miss or misrank.

CAGE-IN's capabilities as deployed at Black Pearl include:

- **Multi-Dataset Integration** — Airborne magnetics, TDEM, geological mapping, and historical records unified within a single constrained interpretation framework.
- **Geologically Constrained Gridding** — Processing confined strictly within geological domain boundaries, eliminating cross-domain distortion of conductive signatures.
- **Enhanced Anomaly Contrast** — Subtle conductors invisible in standard processing are surfaced and ranked by confidence tier for ground verification.
- **Drill-Ready Target Package** — Outputs include GIS-compatible target coordinates, confidence classifications, and written geological context for QP disclosure.

## Scope of Work

AIRE's work program at Black Pearl will encompass the following activities in support of Graphano's upcoming ground exploration season:

- Integration of airborne magnetic and TDEM datasets within a CAGE-IN geological constraint framework
- Enhancement of geophysical contrast to isolate discrete conductive zones along the six-kilometre trend
- Cross-referencing of geophysical targets against historical exploration records and geological mapping
- Generation of ranked, confidence-classified drill and ground-follow-up targets
- Delivery of a complete CAGE-IN report with GIS-compatible outputs for use by Graphano's Qualified Person

Ground activities planned by Graphano — including prospecting, geological mapping, stripping, trenching, channel and grab sampling, and ground geophysical surveys — will be informed directly by AIRE's target prioritization ahead of first-phase drilling.

## Significance of the Project

Graphite is a designated critical mineral and a foundational material for lithium-ion battery anodes, fuel cell components, and a range of advanced energy technologies. The Black Pearl project sits within a historically active graphite region of Québec — a jurisdiction with established infrastructure, a strong exploration track record, and proximity to Canada's only currently producing graphite mine. AIRE views this engagement as an opportunity to demonstrate CAGE-IN's applicability to battery-metal graphite targets at meaningful scale.

With seasonal conditions improving and ground exploration scheduled to commence in the coming weeks, AIRE is proceeding with data integration and expects to deliver its target prioritization report on a timeline aligned with Graphano's field program.
